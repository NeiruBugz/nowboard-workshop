# Product Roadmap: Nowboard

_This roadmap outlines our strategic direction based on customer needs and business goals. It focuses on the "what" and "why," not the technical "how."_

---

### Phase 1

_The highest priority features that form the core foundation of the product — the minimum a team needs to replace their daily standup._

- [x] **Team & Account Essentials**
  - [x] **Sign-Up & Login:** Let users create an account and sign in securely via email + password.
  - [x] **Create or Join a Team:** Let the first user create a team workspace and invite teammates via a shared link, so everyone lands on the same board.

- [ ] **Core Card Posting**
  - [ ] **Post a Status Card:** A one-line card with a status (working on / blocked / done) and an optional link, postable in seconds.
  - [ ] **Edit or Delete Own Card:** Let authors correct typos or remove stale cards without friction.

- [ ] **The Shared Board**
  - [ ] **Glanceable Team View:** A single board grouped by teammate, showing each person's current cards at a glance.
  - [ ] **Today-Focused Surface:** The default view emphasizes the current day so the board stays current and relevant.

---

### Phase 2

_Once teams can post and glance, we layer in lightweight collaboration so the board becomes a place to nudge and unblock — not just broadcast._

- [ ] **Lightweight Collaboration**
  - [ ] **Emoji Reactions on Cards:** One-tap reactions (👀 🙌 🚀) for quick acknowledgment and encouragement.
  - [ ] **Short Threaded Replies:** A small reply thread under each card so teammates can offer help or ask a quick question without leaving the board.

- [ ] **Polish & Adoption**
  - [ ] **Notifications for Replies & Reactions:** Let authors know when someone engages with their card — enough signal, no noise.
  - [ ] **Keyboard-First Posting:** A fast keyboard shortcut to open the "post a card" input so posting a card takes under 30 seconds.

---

### Phase 3

_Features planned for future consideration. Priority and scope may be refined based on feedback from teams using Phase 1 and 2._

- [ ] **History & Discoverability**
  - [ ] **Yesterday View:** A simple way to see what the team posted the previous working day.
  - [ ] **Search Past Cards:** Let users find cards they or teammates posted in the past week.

- [ ] **Deferred (explicitly not v1)**
  - [ ] **Slack Integration:** Post cards from Slack and mirror board updates to a channel.
  - [ ] **Daily Digest:** An end-of-day summary of what the team worked on and shipped.
  - [ ] **Mobile Application:** Native mobile app for posting on the go.
  - [ ] **Analytics & Reporting:** Throughput, activity, or team-health dashboards.
  - [ ] **Cross-Team Rollups:** Org-wide views spanning multiple team boards.
