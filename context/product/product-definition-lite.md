# Nowboard — Summary

## Vision

Give small, distributed or hybrid engineering teams (3–10 people) ambient visibility into what everyone's working on — without a daily standup meeting, without Slack scrollback, and without Jira-style ticket overhead.

## Target Audience

Small engineering teams of 3–10 distributed or hybrid developers who want lightweight async awareness instead of synchronous ceremony.

## Core Features

- Post short status cards (working on / blocked / done) with optional links.
- Shared glanceable team board grouped by person.
- Emoji reactions and short threaded replies on cards.
